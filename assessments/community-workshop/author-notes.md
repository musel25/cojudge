# Community Workshop author notes

The Cooper Union Makerspace uses a sign-out form to track tools and material that people take. Its stock includes acrylic, wood, and wire. UCSB Library supplies limited amounts of materials for projects. These examples informed the revised supply desk, pack sizes, and project allowance. [Cooper Union inventory and sign-out](https://makerspace.cooper.edu/inventory) · [UCSB Makerspace FAQ](https://www.library.ucsb.edu/makerspace-faqs)

In the exercise, the desk and a lead agree on an allowance before the lead records it. The owner role, ordered changes, and consolidation of duplicate project records are original rules. The ledger accounts for packs set aside for a team. Staff handle physical stock separately.

The prose review keeps the original Python methods, cases, answers, and judging rules.
