## Learn

A divisor divides a number with no remainder. For example, 2 is a divisor of 6 because 6 % 2 is 0.

```python
print(6 % 2)  # 0
```

This code prints:

```text
0
```

## Your task

Read n. Count its positive divisors from 1 through n. Show the count. The test values use 1 through 500.

**Example 1**

Input:

```text
6
```

Result:

```text
4
```

Why: The positive divisors of 6 are 1, 2, 3, and 6. There are four.

**Example 2**

Input:

```text
1
```

Result:

```text
1
```

Why: The only positive divisor of 1 is 1.
