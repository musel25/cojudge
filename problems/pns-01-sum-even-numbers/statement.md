## Learn

A for loop repeats code. range(1, n + 1) gives the numbers from 1 through n. Use % 2 to find even numbers.

**Idea en español:** Un ciclo for repite instrucciones. range() da los números que recorre.

```python
for number in range(1, 4):
    print(number)
```
